Welcome to Andhra Pradesh

Explore the beauty, culture, food and famous destinations of Andhra Pradesh.

Famous Places

Tirupati

Tirupati is famous for the Sri Venkateswara Temple and is one of India's important pilgrimage destinations.

Visakhapatnam

Visakhapatnam is a beautiful coastal city known for its beaches, hills and scenic views.

Araku Valley

Araku Valley is known for its beautiful mountains, waterfalls, forests and coffee plantations.

Amaravati

Amaravati is known for its Buddhist heritage and historical importance.

Famous Andhra Food

Andhra Meals

Traditional Andhra meals are known for their variety of dishes and spicy flavors.

Pulihora

Pulihora is a popular rice dish prepared with tamarind, spices and peanuts.

Gongura Pachadi

Gongura pachadi is a famous Andhra dish prepared using sorrel leaves and spices.

Culture of Andhra Pradesh

Andhra Pradesh has a rich cultural heritage with traditional dance, music, festivals, arts and crafts.


Kuchipudi is one of the famous classical dance forms associated with Andhra Pradesh.
